    • Overview: Peaceland is a role-playing game set in a fictionalized post-conflict country, using small player choices to explore how memory and empathy can be tools for peacebuilding rather than division. It's a grant-supported, multi-year Vertically Integrated Project at RIT — I led art direction during the Summer 2024 cohort, and successor teams have continued building on that foundation into an active pre-production phase.

    The project has since been presented at the Serious Play Conference (2025 & 2026) and Narrascope (2024, Strong Museum), and continues to run on an agile, sprint-based workflow across interdisciplinary student cohorts.
