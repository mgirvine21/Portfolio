InBody

  • Procreate
  • Visual Studio
  • GitHub

    • Play the part of a little white blood cell working to solve puzzles and cure the sickness inside the body.

    Overview: InBody is a puzzle game developed over 7 months to raise awareness for mental health and self-care. Players take on the role of a white blood cell navigating through the body, solving pathfinding puzzles to cure illness from within. The project was built with a larger team in Unity and shipped publicly on Itch.io.

    Play on Itch.io

    Key Features

    • Pathfinding puzzle mechanics
    • Body-as-environment level design
    • Mental health awareness theme
    • 7-month multi-discipline production
    • Shipped publicly on Itch.io
