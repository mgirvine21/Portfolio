About

Designer and developer, in equal parts

I'm a versatile game artist and developer with a background in art direction, C# / Unity development, and interactive media. I like working at the seam between a game's visual language and the systems that bring it to life — art direction one day, gameplay code the next.

I've spent the last four years directing art for student-led studio teams, teaching game design to 30+ students at a time, and shipping playable prototypes under real deadlines. I'm looking for a team that wants both halves of that skill set.
