✦ Peaceland ✦

LEAD ARTIST 3D ARTIST CONCEPT ARTIST

Peaceland is a vertically integrated student project developed across multiple cohorts, with successor teams actively building on the original design foundation. I owned art direction from initial concept through production, establishing the visual language and pipeline standards that continue to support the project today.

✦ BugBox ✦

A holographic idle simulation for the Looking Glass display: a virtual ant farm where players customize modular low-poly bugs, observe emergent behaviors, and shape creature interactions in a playful terrarium sandbox. I developed interchangeable bug assets, UI assets, and AI pathing systems for bug movement.

  • Learn More

✦ Custom Graphics Engine ✦

A fully functional real-time 3D graphics engine built from scratch in C++ using DirectX 11 , developed over the course of an advanced graphics programming course . The engine supports a complete rendering pipeline including physically-based shading, dynamic lighting, shadow mapping, and a custom content pipeline. Constructed incrementally from a blank Visual Studio project to a playable, hardware-accelerated build.

ABOUT ME

MG Irvine is a passionate and versatile Designer and Developer with a strong background in art, C# & Unity, and interactive media. I thrive on creating engaging game experiences through a blend of creative design and technical development. I’ve had the opportunity to collaborate with talented teams on game jams, internships, and academic projects, and I’m always excited to work with others to create something innovative and impactful. If you’re looking for a driven, collaborative designer with a passion for game development, let’s connect!
