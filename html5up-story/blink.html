A holographic idle simulation for the Looking Glass display: a virtual ant farm where players customize modular low-poly bugs, observe emergent behaviors, and shape creature interactions in a playful terrarium sandbox. I developed interchangeable bug assets, UI assets, and AI pathing systems for bug movement.

Key Features: Bug Customization · Omnidirectional AI Pathfinding · Interactive Terrarium · Holographic Multi-Angle Viewing · Day/Night Cycles · Biome Variety

Overview: Bug Box is a holographic idle simulation built for the Looking Glass display, designed to mimic the wonder of a virtual ant farm. Players customize modular low-poly bugs, observe emergent behaviors within an interactive terrarium, and influence creature interactions in a playful sandbox environment.
